# PARA in one page (newsletter reprint)

Reprinted with permission from Tiago Forte's original PARA method write-up; this is the
newsletter's copy, the canonical article lives on his own site.

Four folders, one question each. Projects: does it have a specific outcome you can close?
Areas: is it a responsibility you maintain with no end date? Resources: is it reference
material tied to neither? Archive: is it finished or frozen? Forte's point is that the
folders sort by actionability, not by subject, and that most people misfile projects as areas
because they never write down the outcome that would close them.
