# Keeping spring arrival dates for the local patch

A standing practice, not a project: log the first date each migrant species is seen on the
local patch every spring, year after year. Over time the series shows shifts of a week or
more, and it is the quickest cross-check when a published range map or arrival window looks
wrong. Needs only a dated list per species; the value is in never stopping.
