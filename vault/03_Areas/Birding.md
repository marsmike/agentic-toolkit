# Birding

Ongoing responsibility, no end date — unlike [[Field-Guide-Project]], which is scoped to a
finished manuscript. This area is where day-to-day patch birding, life-list upkeep, and gear notes
live; the field guide draws on it but doesn't own it.

## What lives here

- Local patch visit notes and seasonal arrival dates, used as a cross-check against
  [[Reference-Library|the field guide's reference library]] when a range claim looks off.
- Optics and field gear notes — separate from anything in [[Home-Network-Administration]], though
  both are "ongoing area, no closable outcome" in the PARA sense; see [[PARA-Method]].
- Species familiarity that eventually becomes a [[Species-Accounts-Workflow|species account]],
  once there's enough material to write one.

## Relationship to the field-guide project

Every account in the field guide starts as something noticed here first. The project exists
because this area accumulated enough material to be worth publishing — a common PARA pattern: a
project is often just an area's backlog reaching critical mass.
