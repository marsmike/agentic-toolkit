# Illustration Sourcing

The current bottleneck on the [[Field-Guide-Project|field guide]]. Two sourcing paths per
species, chosen per-account rather than uniformly:

- **Original illustration** — commissioned once the account's text is stable (see
  [[Species-Accounts-Workflow]] step 5). Slower, better for species where field marks are subtle
  and a photo can mislead by lighting or angle.
- **Licensed photo** — faster, fine for common species with unambiguous marks. Licensing takes a
  request through the same publisher contact tracked in [[Publisher-Outreach-Log]].

## Current state

~70 accounts have final art, ~40 are commissioned and in progress, the rest are waiting on text to
stabilize. Two licensed-photo requests are stuck pending a reply — see this week's
[[Weekly-Review]].
