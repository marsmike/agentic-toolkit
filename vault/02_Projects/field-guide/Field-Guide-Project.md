# Field Guide Project

A print-ready field guide to the birds of Alex's home region: ~180 species accounts, range maps,
and identification notes aimed at intermediate birders. Distinct from [[Birding]] (the ongoing
hobby area this project draws on) by having a specific, closable outcome — a finished manuscript.

## Scope

- 180 species accounts, organized by family, each following the
  [[Species-Accounts-Workflow|species-account workflow]].
- Original illustrations or licensed photos per [[Illustration-Sourcing]].
- One regional publisher under discussion — see [[Publisher-Outreach-Log]].
- Source material tracked in [[Reference-Library]].

## Status

Manuscript ~40% drafted by species count. Illustration sourcing is the current bottleneck (see
[[Illustration-Sourcing]]). Weekly cadence tracked in [[Weekly-Review]] (this project's, not the
[[Home-Lab-Migration|home-lab]] one of the same name — see [[Test-Corpus-Map]] if that's
confusing on purpose).
